# Casa de IA Cyber Lab — BLSI

Status: proposed foundation; not a deployed capability.

## Charter
Research defensive business-logic security on explicitly authorized assets. Synthetic data and offline fixtures are the default. No third-party exploitation, real payments, credentials, customer data or unremediated findings in public artifacts. External access and sensitive actions remain governed by the existing Constitution, Policy Engine and Ethics Engine; no new authority is granted by this specification.

## Architecture
BLSI is a proposed competency, not a separate agent. Mission Engine schedules bounded audits; Agent Orchestrator proposes actions; Integration Gateway validates tool boundaries; Ethics Engine enforces grants and approvals. MCP Guardian integration requires a separate compatibility review. Evidence storage must respect tenant isolation and existing audit policies.

## Invariants
- Payment entitlement requires authoritative, verified settlement and matching tenant, currency and amount.
- Repeated event identifiers cannot duplicate financial effects.
- Promotion eligibility is checked server-side; usage limits are atomic.
- Workflow transitions must be explicitly allowed and authorized.
- Cross-tenant access is denied; unknown or missing evidence is inconclusive, never a pass.
- Revocation removes future entitlement; audit evidence remains appropriately protected.

## Research protocol
Use deterministic fixtures, versioned scenario IDs, expected outcomes and reproducible seeds. Report false positives, false negatives, coverage and limitations. Classify findings using CWE and applicable OWASP guidance; severity requires contextual review. Keep real findings private until remediation and authorized disclosure.

## Delivery gates
1. Offline invariant evaluator and positive/negative tests.
2. Synthetic scenario catalog: 20 payment, 20 isolation, 15 entitlement, 15 workflow, 10 concurrency, 10 promotion, 10 audit scenarios (target, not implemented coverage).
3. Independent review and constitutional regression checks.
4. Authorized staging pilot; no production changes or financial effects.
5. Research report and eligibility verification for external research programs. No partnership or acceptance is implied.

## Acceptance
No runtime integration until test evidence, authorization boundaries, resource limits, and fail-closed behavior are reviewed. No migrations or new paid services are required for this foundation.
